## Differentiation

1. Asset-to-game workflow: upload a weapon, character, vehicle, creature, prop, photo, drawing, or concept and progress through inspection, repair, style selection, rigging, animation, optimization, validation, and game integration.
2. Agent Reach: specialized agents can operate on the real project rather than returning only advice.
3. Intelligent Roleplay: persistent NPC memory, personality, relationships, schedules, factions, dialogue and world-aware decisions.
4. Remix & Evolve: remix is permission-controlled and preserves attribution/licensing lineage.
5. Mechanic-first templates: survival, creature survival, tactical FPS, battle royale, extraction, roleplay and other patterns without copying protected game expression.
